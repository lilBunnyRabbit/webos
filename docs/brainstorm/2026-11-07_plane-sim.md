# Plane sim: side-view takeoff and landing
**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Participants:** owner, Claude

## Context
Owner, 11-07: "In the same style and ideas as the drive hub and harbor i would want a 2d plane simulator. This wouldn't be a top down view but i guess side view? Grill me, come up with ideas, lets make a solid plan".

What exists today:
- Harbour (`src/lib/apps/user/harbour/`) sets the pattern: feel-based spec solved into coefficients (`core/boats.ts`, `core/boat.ts`), graded contacts, drivable hub with a shed picker, 8 levels + random + daily, autopilot proof and par `ceil(1.4 × autopilot + 8)` (`core/autopilot.ts`, `core/planner.ts`).
- Shared 2D pieces in `$kit/utils/play2d/` (geometry, collision, camera, loop, seed, palette) and kit components (round card, key hint, speed chip, steering wheel).
- Harbour's throttle lever (`harbour/lever.svelte`, `core/lever.ts`) would get a second user.

## Topics discussed

### Core loop (decided)
Takeoff and landing, both first-class. Levels mix short-field and obstacle takeoffs with landings; a level may be a full circuit (take off, then land). The landing touchdown is graded like Harbour's fenders.
- Rejected: landing-only (owner wants takeoffs too); full route missions (long levels, hard proof); free flight + challenges (weak structure).

### Flight model (decided)
Real longitudinal model: lift and drag over angle of attack with a real stall, pitch moment with static stability, elevator with lag, trim, flaps, ground effect, gear contact springs. Specs by feel (stall speed, glide ratio, climb rate, flare float, roll-out, pitch rate) solved into coefficients, as in Harbour.
- Rejected: semi-arcade pitch-rate control (loses the energy game); real + assists setting (more surface, later maybe).
- Risk carried from Harbour: a retune can break level proofs; keep proof margins wide.

### Camera and scale (decided)
Real-scale world; camera zooms out with height and in near the ground so the flare reads large. Optional approach-profile inset.
- Rejected: compressed world (numbers stop being real); fixed real scale + HUD (cartoonish).

### Hub (decided)
Side-view home airfield: taxi left/right to a hangar (plane picker, like Harbour's shed) and level gates along the apron; take off from the runway to fly free over the home field.
- Rejected: map menu (breaks house style); flyable hub that needs a landing per pick (slow).

### Planes (decided)
All four in scope, one feel each:
- trainer taildragger: stall ~18 m/s, floaty, wind-sensitive; the starter.
- bush STOL: big flaps, stall ~13 m/s, ~40 m roll-out, steep approaches, hill strips, obstacle takeoffs.
- airliner (~70 t): slow spool and pitch, spoilers, reverse, long roll-out; rewards planning, like the coaster.
- glider: no engine, energy, thermals and sink, one go at the landing.

### Controls (decided)
W/S or arrows for elevator, springing back to trim; T trims. Shift/Ctrl or the Harbour lever detents for throttle; F/V flaps; B brakes. Touch: throttle lever on the left, spring-back pitch pad on the right, flap and brake buttons. Mouse: vertical drag for pitch.
- Rejected: mouse yoke (pointer lock fights the window UI); hold-only without trim (long climbs hold a key).

### Weather (decided)
Head/tail wind per level, gusts on final, windshear, plus thermals and sinking air (needed for the glider).

### Scoring (decided)
Three stars: done, gentle, under par.
- Landing done: stopped on the runway.
- Takeoff done: cleared the obstacle, no tail strike, no stall.
- Touchdown sink grades: kiss <0.6 m/s, firm <1.5 m/s, hard ≥3 m/s (a crash).
- Par from the autopilot proof, as in Harbour.
- Rejected: points score (breaks the house stars style).

### Name (decided)
Airfield, id `airfield`. Covers the hub, takeoffs, landings and gliders. Rejected: Short Final, Flare (landing-biased), Circuit (reads as racing).

### Levels (decided)
Per-plane tracks, five levels each, plus random and daily for any plane. Rejected: shared scaled levels (a STOL strip is meaningless for an airliner, a glider can't take off); mixed.

### Build order (decided)
Feel first, then waves, nothing shipped before wave 3:
1. model + trainer + hub + free flight; owner reports feel;
2. STOL, airliner, glider, weather; owner tunes feel;
3. levels, autopilot proof, par, random, daily; ship experimental.

### Shell (decided)
Nothing beyond `app launch airfield`.

### Details decided by Claude (owner may override)
- Glider levels start released at height; in the hub it goes up on a winch launch.
- Harbour's lever moves to the kit (second user, repo rule) in a `refactor:` commit first.
- Flight model in the app, not the kit: no state shape shared with Harbour's planar hull.
- Experimental (ADR-097) until feel-tuned.

### Feel reference (noted 11-07)
Owner: "I love playing simple planes 1 and especially 2 (although completely different game concept to this)". Taken as qualities only, not its concept (no craft building) or look: physics that is honest yet readable, so a mistake is the player's and is obvious why; planes that feel weighty and respond at once; free flight that is fun on its own, between goals; crashes that read clearly. Harbour's Sea of Thieves reference was used the same way.

## Key takeaways
- Same pattern as Harbour, side view: feel specs to coefficients, graded touchdown, hub with picker, autopilot proof.
- Bigger than Harbour, so it is built in waves with owner feel checks between them.
