## Topics discussed

Its own app, not a Drive Hub game. Rejected: `games/harbour/` in Drive Hub (shares the frame, not the model; less visible in the store).

### Code sharing (decided)

Drive Hub's imports are rewired; Harbour imports from the kit. Lunar Lander may adopt it later.
- Rejected: copying into the app (copies drift; repo rule).

### Name (decided)
Harbour, id `harbour`. Covers the marina, lock and ferry later.

### First cut (decided)
P1 from the research: RIB + 10 m yacht; alongside + box berth; momentum, keel drag, rudder with prop wash, prop walk, engine/gear/rudder lags, rest dead band; calm water; 8 levels + random + daily.

### Throttle (decided)
Both modes, as a setting. Default is a single lever with detents that stays where it's left (W/S one notch, Space = neutral; touch: a vertical lever). The setting switches to hold-to-thrust (hold W ahead, release = neutral).

### Boat physics home (decided)
In the app: `harbour/core/boat.ts`, with the integrator as a small pure function so it can move to the kit when F1 slip or Lunar Lander needs a rigid body. Rejected: a generic body in the kit now (no second user yet).

### Contact (decided)
Soft fenders, a spring-damper at the contact point. A touch is graded by approach speed at first touch:
- kiss, under 0.15 m/s: free;
- bump: costs the gentle star;
- hard, over 0.5 m/s: ends the round.

Hard walls and moored boats halve the limits. Collision returns depth (MTV).
- Rejected: points only (no tension).
- Rejected: the car rule (alongside is near impossible).

### Docked and scoring (decided)
A boat is docked when it is in the berth, within 8°, stopped and held for 1 s; then the auto crew puts the lines on. Stars: docked, gentle, on time (par). Precision ≥ 95 % is Perfect. Gear changes don't count.
- Rejected: a points total (off-pattern with the stars).
- Rejected: a Line button (later, with tethers).

### Proof (decided)
A geometric check (berth width, the A* planner with the boat's turning circle) plus a closed-loop autopilot in the real physics. The autopilot proves the level and sets the par.
- Rejected: geometry only.
- Rejected: recorded replays.

### Assists (decided)
None. Owner picked no predicted-track ghost: judging momentum is the skill.

### Home screen (decided)
A sail-around hub, a small harbour you steer about:
- each level is a numbered pair of gate buoys (its stars on a sign); stop past it and a start card opens; the level loads as its own scene;
- Random and Daily are two more gates;
- the boat shed swaps RIB ↔ yacht;
- the physics is real, contacts bounce, and nothing fails in the hub.

Rejected: a chart menu and a level list.

### Shell (decided)
A `daily` prop (ADR-050) for Game Hub's daily row, and `daily_rank`. No commands, no level or boat props.

### Units (decided)
Knots on the speed chip, metres for distances.

### Kit path (lead's call)
The kit already has `src/lib/kit/games/` (daily, grid, plays). play2d goes there as `$kit/games/play2d/`, with its components in `$kit/components/play2d/`.

## Key takeaways
- No dependency: planck.js and matter.js would only save the contact code and give nothing for hydrodynamics.
- Feel-based boat specs with a tested `coefficients(spec)` keep the tuning in words a player feels.
