## The rules

The numbers beside each row and above each column are its runs of filled cells, in order, with at least one empty cell between them. Fill the cells so every row and column matches its numbers, and the picture appears.

In a colour puzzle each number has a colour. Runs of the same colour need a gap between them; runs of different colours may touch.

## Controls

- **Click or tap** a cell to fill it, and again to clear it. **Drag** to paint a straight line.
- Mark a cell you know stays empty with a cross: right-click or Shift-click it, or pick **Cross** (the × in a colour puzzle) and tap.
- In a colour puzzle, pick a colour from the swatches or press 1, 2, 3 and so on.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move, Space or Enter fills, X crosses, Backspace clears. Hold Shift with the arrows to paint as you go.
- Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes. F2 starts a new puzzle, Ctrl+O opens a puzzle file.
- On a narrow window, boards of 15×15 and up have a zoom button.

**Hint** (H) first fixes a cell you got wrong, then marks one more cell. Best times count only solves without hints.

## Puzzles

- Pick black and white or colour, and a size from 5×5 to 20×20. Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone, no guessing.
- The puzzle in progress is kept for next time. Statistics keep the solved count and best time per size.
- The daily is a 10×10 black-and-white puzzle, the same for everyone. Play it from Game Hub, or with `nonogram daily` in the Terminal.

## Making your own

- **Create a puzzle** (the pencil) opens the editor. Draw the picture and its numbers build around it. Set the width, height and colours. E picks the eraser, and right-click erases.
- The bar above the grid says whether the puzzle has only one answer, and whether logic alone gets there.
- **From an image…** turns a picture into a puzzle you can tweak.
- **Play-test** tries it out, **Save** (Ctrl+S) keeps it as a `.nonogram` file. Open one from your files or with Ctrl+O; the pencil edits it.
