## Scanning

- **Add photos** opens your phone's camera or your photo picker; pick several at once if you like. **From files…** takes pictures already in WebOS. You can also drop or paste pictures into the window, or use **Open with** on a picture.
- Click or tap a page to open it. Drag a corner onto the page's corner; on the keyboard, Tab to a corner and move it with the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps).
- **Find edges** puts the found corners back. **Whole photo** keeps the entire picture.
- **Color**, **Gray** or **B&W** sets the look of that page. New pages start with the one you picked last.
- **Preview** shows the straightened page as it goes into the PDF. **Done** goes back to the pages.

## Saving

- The arrows under each page change the order; the bin removes it.
- **Save PDF** asks where to save, starting in Documents. Every page is A4 wide.
- Pages are only kept until you close the window, so save before you go. Nothing is uploaded: it all happens on your device.

## Tips

- Shoot the page on a darker surface, with some table showing around it. A white page on a white desk, or a page that fills the whole photo, can't be found, and you place the corners yourself.
- A page turned more than 45° in the photo can come out sideways. Drag the corners round to fix it.
