## Playing

- Press **Start**. Each card names a word; press **Go** and the 20-second clock starts.
- Draw with the mouse, a pen or a finger. Each time you lift and pause, the AI takes a look and says what it sees under the board.
- When one of its guesses is your word, you move on to the next card. If time runs out, a guess still on its way counts if it's right.
- **Undo** (Ctrl+Z, Cmd+Z on a Mac) takes back the last line, **Clear** wipes the board, **Skip** gives up on the word.
- With the board focused, Enter or Space presses Start and Go.

## Scoring

Your score is how many of the six words the AI recognised. A tie goes to the faster total time. Your best round shows in the status bar.

## After the round

**See drawings** shows every drawing with the AI's last guess. Save any of them as a PNG. Drawings you don't save are gone when the window closes.

## Tips

- The AI picks from a fixed list of everyday things. Draw the object plainly, the way a child would.
- The first guess on a new computer can wait for Chrome to download its AI model, while the clock keeps running.
