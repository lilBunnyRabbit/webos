## One colour, six sections

The colour at the top is the current colour, and every section works on it. Click or tap any swatch, in any section, to make it the current colour. The copy buttons beside it copy the colour as written, and its hex.

## Pick

- **Pick from screen** takes any pixel on your screen, inside WebOS or outside it. Escape cancels. It only shows in browsers that support it.
- **Open image…**, **From device…**, dropping an image or pasting one puts a picture in the window. Drag over it to aim, with a magnifier on the exact pixel, and let go to pick. With the picture focused, arrow keys move one pixel (Shift for ten) and Enter picks.
- **Camera** shows a live view. Click or tap the view to freeze it and pick that spot, or press **Freeze frame** to pick from the still afterwards.
- **Type it** takes a hex, a colour name, any CSS colour function or any model's own syntax, like `hsv(30 50% 80%)`. Press Enter to apply, Escape to undo your typing.
- **Adjust** gives a slider per channel in the model you pick, plus alpha. The strip under each slider shows where it leads.

## The other sections

- **Convert** lists the colour in all 70 models. Type in the filter to find one; every value pastes back into Pick.
- **Contrast** shows your colour as text on a background, with its ratio and pass or fail badges. **Swap** exchanges text and background.
- **Vision** shows how the colour, or the whole open palette, looks with each type of colour blindness. The **Severity** slider goes from a mild form to full.
- **Harmony** builds schemes by turning the hue, and **Steps** blends to a second colour in 3 to 12 steps. Results differ with the hue space you pick.

## Palettes

- **New palette** starts one from the current colour. It's saved as a `.palette` file in Documents, and every change saves as you go.
- **Add current colour** adds a swatch. With a palette open, each scheme in Harmony gets **Add to palette**.
- Click or tap a palette's name to rename it, type in a swatch's name field to name it, and use the bin to remove it. A palette holds up to 256 colours.
- **Open…** picks a palette from any folder, and a `.palette` file opened in File Explorer opens here. **From device…** copies one into Documents.
- Each window keeps its own colour and section, and gets them back when the session is restored.
