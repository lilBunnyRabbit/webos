## The tour

The terminal on the left types a short tour and opens each window in turn: Who I am, Work, Projects, How I build, then Contact. Sit back, or take over at any time.

- **Type anything** and the tour stops, with the rest of the windows open. The control under the terminal skips, resumes or replays it.
- On a phone the windows form one long page, and the steps under the terminal jump between them.

## The terminal

- `help` lists the commands. Try `work`, `projects`, `how`, `contact` or `tour`.
- Press **/** to search, or type any word: it searches jobs, projects and principles.
- Suggestions appear as you type. Arrow keys pick one, Tab takes it, Enter runs what you typed.
- On a wide screen, `tidy` puts dragged windows back and `close all` clears the desk.

## Around the desk

- In **How I build**, click a principle and the jobs and projects that show it light up.
- Click a project's picture to replay it.
- **Contact** writes an email in your own mail app. Send, or Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac), opens it there; nothing is sent from here.
