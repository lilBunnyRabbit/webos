## Two colours

- **Left click** draws with the primary colour, **right click** with the secondary. On a touchscreen you always draw with the primary.
- Click a swatch to make it primary, right-click it for secondary. **X** swaps the two.
- Rectangles and ellipses come as **Outline**, **Fill** or **Both**. **Both** fills with the other colour.

## Tools and keys

- **P** pencil, **B** brush, **E** eraser, **F** fill, **I** colour picker, **T** text, **H** pan.
- **L** line, **R** rectangle, **O** ellipse. Hold **Shift** for a square, a circle, or a line at 45° steps.
- A pen's pressure changes the brush width.
- Ctrl+scroll zooms. On a phone, the toolbox button shows and hides the tools.

## Files

- Ctrl+O opens, Ctrl+S saves, Ctrl+Shift+S saves as. Undo with Ctrl+Z, redo with Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y.
- The extension picks the format: PNG, JPEG or WebP. A new drawing saves as PNG.
- The new-file button sets the canvas size and background. **Resize** keeps the drawing, **New** starts blank.
