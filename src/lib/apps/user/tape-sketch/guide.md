The drawing doesn't need to be to scale: the numbers you type are what count.

## Draw the shape

- **Corners** (C): tap each corner. Tap the first corner again, or press **Close shape**, to close it. **Finish** leaves it open.
- **Walk** (W): tap where to start, type a length, then pick a direction. Each step draws one wall. On a keyboard the arrows walk, and Home, Page Up, End and Page Down go diagonally.
- The **magnet** button snaps lines to 45°, iso, 15° or your own angles. Tap it to switch or turn it off.

## Measure

- Tap a line's number, or its edge, to open the numpad. Type the length.
- The arrows next to the number (or ← → and Tab) step to the next length still to measure. ✓ or Enter closes the numpad; **Clear** removes a length.
- **Measure** (D) adds a dimension between any two points. **Marker** (M) pins a note on a point, like a socket or a height. **Note** (N) puts text anywhere.
- The units button switches between mm, cm, m and inches.

## Check it

A closed shape shows whether its lengths add up, suggests a missing length, and gives the area and perimeter. **Redraw to scale** reshapes it to match the numbers. Turn **Check lengths** off for a box drawn in 3D.

## Editing and moving around

- **Select** (V): drag a corner, a dimension or a note to move it. Delete or Backspace removes what's selected.
- Drag empty space to pan. Pinch to zoom and twist two fingers to turn the sketch. On a desktop, scroll pans and Ctrl+scroll zooms. Ctrl+0 fits the sketch.
- Undo with Ctrl+Z, redo with Ctrl+Shift+Z.

## Saving

The **File** menu saves .tapesketch files (Ctrl+S) and exports a PNG or SVG.
