## Setting up

- **New bracket**, give it a name and pick a format: **Knockout**, **Double**, **Round robin** or **Race cup**.
- List the players one per line, best first. The order is the seeding: in a knockout the top seeds meet last.
- Each format has its own options below: a third-place match, a grand final reset, playing everyone once or twice, points, races and group sizes.

## Entering results

- **Knockout and double:** click or tap the player who won. The winner moves on to the next round. Click the winner again to clear a result while the next round hasn't been played.
- **Round robin:** click or tap the winner of a fixture, or the middle button for a draw. You can also type the scores; the table then shows the score difference.
- **Race cup:** tap the players in the order they finished (the last one fills in by itself), then **Save race**. **Back** takes the last pick away.

## Your brackets

Every bracket is a `.bracket` file in Documents, saved after each result. Click or tap its name at the top to rename it, and the back arrow to see all your brackets. You can also open a `.bracket` file from your device.
