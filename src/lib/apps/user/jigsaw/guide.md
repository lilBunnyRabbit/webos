## Getting a picture

- Click or tap **Open picture…** to pick an image from your files, or paste or drop one onto the window.
- You can also open an image from the file manager with Jigsaw.
- Choose 24, 48, 100 or 200 pieces in the toolbar. A small window offers only the counts that fit; make it bigger for more pieces.

## Putting it together

- Drag a piece to move it. Drop it close to a matching neighbour and the two snap together and move as one from then on.
- Drop a piece or group near its spot in the frame and it locks in place.
- Pieces never need turning; they all face the right way.
- The puzzle is done when every piece is joined.

## Moving around

- Drag the empty table to pan. On a phone, pinch to zoom.
- With a mouse, scroll to pan and Ctrl+scroll to zoom. The zoom buttons and **Fit everything** are in the toolbar.

## Helpers

- **Edge pieces only** hides every group without an edge piece, so you can build the border first.
- **Preview in the frame** shows the picture faintly where the pieces go.
- **New cut** cuts the same picture again with new shapes and shuffles the pieces.

## Saving

A puzzle from a file in your files is kept as you go and comes back next time. A pasted or dropped picture isn't kept.
