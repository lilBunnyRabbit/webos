## The goal

Clear lines and keep the middle free. The game ends when a new piece has no room to enter in the middle of the board.

## The board

- The two diagonals split the square into four zones, one per wall. Each wall is a floor.
- Every piece starts in the middle and falls toward one wall. The arrow beside it in **Next** shows which.
- A line runs along a wall from corner to corner. Fill it and it clears, and that wall's zone slides toward the wall.
- A corner cell belongs to two lines, one for each wall it touches, and clears with either.
- One piece can clear lines against two walls at once.
- The middle four cells never clear. A piece left there stays there.

## Controls

Arrow keys act on the board as you see it, so what they do depends on where the piece is heading:

- The two arrows **across** its way steer it. Hold one to keep moving.
- Its **own** arrow drops it softly.
- The **opposite** arrow rotates it, like X. Z rotates the other way.
- Space drops it all the way. C or Shift holds it for later; a held piece comes back heading for the wall of the piece it swaps with.
- P or Escape pauses. Enter starts, resumes, or plays again.

On a phone, use the pad under the board: Hold, Rotate left, Rotate right and Drop on top, then steer, soft drop and steer. The arrows turn with the piece. On the board itself, slide a finger across to steer, slide along to drop softly, flick along to drop all the way, and tap to rotate.

## Modes and scoring

- **Cycle:** walls go round, one per piece, and the order turns back every 10 pieces.
- **Random:** every piece gets its own wall.
- **Daily:** the same pieces and walls for everyone, once a day. Game Hub keeps your best score.

Clearing 1, 2, 3 or 4 lines with one piece scores 100, 300, 500 or 800 times the level, and each line past four adds 400 more. Soft drops add 1 a row, hard drops 2. Every 10 lines raises the level, and pieces fall faster. Your best score and lines for each mode are in the side panel, or behind the chart button in a narrow window.
