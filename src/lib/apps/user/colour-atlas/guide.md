## A round

- The big swatch shows the target on the left and your colour on the right. The model's name and its tier sit above the sliders.
- Move the sliders until the two halves match, then press **Submit**. You can't see the target's numbers until you've submitted.
- The strip on each slider shows what that slider would do with the others held, so read the strips rather than the numbers.
- The reveal shows both colours, the target's values and yours, and your score. **Next round** goes on.

## Scoring

- Each round is scored on ΔE, how different the two colours look: 0 is a perfect match. A round is worth up to 100, losing 8 points per unit of ΔE, so a run tops out at 500.
- On the final screen each round gets a square: green for 90 or more, yellow for 70, orange for 40, red below that.

## Keyboard

- Arrow keys move the focused slider, Tab moves to the next one.
- **Enter** submits, then moves on to the next round.

## The atlas

- Finish a round under ΔE 5 to collect that model. **Atlas** in the toolbar shows all 70 slots; a `?` is one you haven't collected yet.
- Click or tap any slot to open its page: tier, family, source, its channels and their ranges, and the route its colours take to sRGB. Each step of the route opens that model's page.
- The **Practice** button on a model's page plays five rounds of just that model. sRGB bytes is never played, but its page still opens.

## Daily and practice

- **Daily** is one attempt: two Familiar models, two Perceptual and one Exotic, the same for everyone. Close the window halfway and it picks up at the next round. Game Hub keeps your results.
- **Practice** plays a new run whenever you press **New run**. The list beside it picks Mixed, or one tier only.
- **Copy result** copies your squares and a link. Whoever opens the link plays the same run.
