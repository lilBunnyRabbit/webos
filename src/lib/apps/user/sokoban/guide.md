## The goal

You're the round piece. Push every crate onto a ringed goal. A crate turns green when it sits on one.

## The rules

- You can only **push** a crate, never pull it, and only one at a time.
- A crate pushed into a corner can never move again. Think before you push; Undo takes a push back.
- Every level can be solved.

## Controls

- **Move** with the arrow keys or WASD, swipe on the board, or tap the arrow pad on a touch screen.
- **Undo** with Ctrl+Z, redo with Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y. On a touch screen they sit next to the arrow pad.
- **R** restarts the level, **F2** starts a new one.

## Scores and daily

The clock starts on your first move. Each level remembers your fewest moves, and the statistics keep the solves and best time per difficulty. The daily is a Medium level, the same for everyone, and Game Hub keeps your results.
