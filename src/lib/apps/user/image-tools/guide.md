## Getting around

Pick a tool from the list on the left, or from the menu in the toolbar on a narrow window or a phone. Each tool keeps its image and settings while you switch to another.

## Bringing images in

- Use the open button, drop files onto the tool, or paste with Ctrl+V.
- Join, Squash and Atlas take many images at once. Compare and Patch take two: the before and the after, or the original and the edited copy.
- In File Explorer, right-click an image and pick Image Tools under **Open with**. It opens on Remove background.

## Saving

- A button ending in "…" asks where to save. Split and Sprites write a new folder of files inside the one you pick.
- **Save** in Fit and Convert writes next to the original, or to Pictures for an image you dropped or pasted. Squash does the same unless you pick a folder under **Save to**.
- Redact always saves a fresh copy, without the original's metadata.

## Working on the picture

In Redact and Patch, pick a tool above the picture:

- **Box** drags a rectangle and **Brush** paints an area: Redact covers it, Patch brings the original back there. **Erase** paints part of an area away again.
- **Select** pans; in Redact a click or tap picks an area to change its effect, in Patch it turns a changed spot on or off.
- Zoom with Ctrl+scroll or a pinch, pan with scroll or the middle mouse button. The percentage button fits the picture again.

Compare and Patch line the two pictures up by themselves. If the match is rough, set the scale and offset by hand or press **Align again**.

## Sprites

- **Detect** finds the grid from the gaps between frames. Drag the grid lines to fix ones that don't line up.
- Click or tap a frame to adjust it, then drag it or its handles. Arrows move it (Shift for 10 px), Alt+arrows resize it, C shrinks it to its visible pixels, R puts it back.
- Double-click a frame, or hold it on a phone, to take it out of the animation or put it back. Delete does the same for the selected frame.
- P plays or pauses the preview, [ and ] step through frames, L shows or hides frame numbers. Zoom with + and -, F fits the sheet, and hold Space to pan.

## From the Terminal

Many tools have a command, like `image fit`, `image split`, `squash`, `poster` or `testimg`. Type `help` in the Terminal to see them all.
