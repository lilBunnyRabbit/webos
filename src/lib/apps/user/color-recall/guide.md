## How a round goes

- Press **Show the first colour**. It fills the window for 5 seconds (2 on Hard), with a bar counting down. Press **I'm ready** to move on early.
- The sliders start on a deliberately wrong colour. Drag them until the big swatch matches what you remember, then press **Lock in**.
- You see the real colour beside yours, both hex codes and your score. **Next colour** starts the next one.

## Scoring

- Each colour scores up to 10, so a game tops out at 50. The score measures how different the two colours look to the eye, not how far apart the slider numbers are.
- On the final screen each colour gets a square: green for 9 or more, yellow for 7, orange for 4, red below that.

## Sliders

- **HSB** gives hue, saturation and brightness. **OKLCH** gives lightness, chroma and hue, which change more evenly to the eye. Switch any time; your colour carries over.

## Keyboard

- Arrow keys move the focused slider, Tab moves to the next one.
- **Enter** does the next step: show the colour, skip the wait, lock in, go on.

## Daily and practice

- **Daily** is one attempt at today's five colours, the same for everyone. Close the window halfway and it picks up where you left off. Game Hub keeps your results.
- **Practice** plays endless new sets, on **Easy 5 s** or **Hard 2 s**. **New puzzle** deals another.
- **Copy result** copies your squares and a link. Whoever opens the link plays the same colours.
