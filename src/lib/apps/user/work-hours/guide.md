## Tracking time

- **Clock in** when you start, and type what you're working on in the field that appears.
- **Clock out** books the session. A session past midnight is split across the days.
- Forgot to clock in? Pick the day in the calendar and use **Add an entry** with a start and end time. Each entry can be edited or deleted.
- Every day has a **Note** for anything worth remembering.

## The month

- The calendar shows hours per day and a total for each week. Arrows switch months, **Today** jumps back.
- **Monthly target** (the target icon) sets the hours you mean to work. The progress shows next to the month's total.
- **Print monthly report** prints a time sheet, or saves it as a PDF.

## More ways in

On a computer, **Add to desktop** puts a widget on the desktop to clock in and out without opening the app. The Terminal works too: `wh in`, `wh out`, `wh status`.
