## Modes

- **Marathon:** play as long as you can. Every 10 lines raises the level, and pieces fall faster. The game ends when the stack reaches the top.
- **Sprint 40:** clear 40 lines as fast as you can. Your time is the score.

## Controls

- **Keyboard:** Left and Right move, Down drops softly, Space drops at once. Up or X rotates, Z rotates back. C or Shift holds a piece for later, once per piece. P or Escape pauses.
- **Touch:** drag on the board to move a piece or pull it down, tap to rotate, flick down to drop. The buttons below the board do the same, plus hold.
- The faint outline shows where the piece will land. The side shows the held piece and the next five.

## Scoring

Clearing more lines at once scores far more: one line is 100, four lines is 800, times the level. Dropping pieces adds a little too.

## Daily

The daily is a Sprint with the same pieces for everyone, and Game Hub keeps your times. Open it from Game Hub, or type `blocks daily` in the Terminal. Your stats and top scores sit beside the board in a wide window, behind the chart button in a narrow one.
