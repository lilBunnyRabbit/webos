## The rules

- Every cell holds a sun or a moon.
- Each row and column has as many suns as moons: three of each on 6×6, four on 8×8.
- Never three of the same next to each other in a row or column.
- Cells joined by **=** match. Cells joined by **×** differ.

Shaded cells are given and can't change. A cell that breaks a rule turns red, and so does a broken sign.

## Controls

- **Click or tap** a cell to cycle it: sun, moon, empty.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move. S or 1 places a sun, M or 2 a moon, Space cycles, Backspace clears.
- Undo with Ctrl+Z, redo with Ctrl+Shift+Z. F2 starts a new puzzle.

## When you're stuck

**Hint** (H) first fixes a wrong mark. Otherwise it places the next cell logic forces and says which rule forces it. Every puzzle has one answer you can reach without guessing.

## Timer and daily

The clock starts with your first move. Best times count only solves without hints. The daily is a 6×6 puzzle, the same for everyone; open it from Game Hub, which keeps your results.
