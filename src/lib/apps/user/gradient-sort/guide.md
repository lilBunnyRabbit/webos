## The goal

Put every tile back in its place so the colours blend smoothly from corner to corner. Tiles with a dot are locked and show you where the gradient goes.

## Swapping

- **Drag** a tile onto another to swap them (mouse).
- Or **click or tap** one tile, then a second one. Click the first tile again, or press Escape, to let go of it.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move around the board, Enter picks a tile, Enter on another swaps them.
- Every swap counts as a move. The clock starts on your first swap.

## Daily and practice

- **Daily** is one Easy gradient a day, the same for everyone, ranked by time in Game Hub. Leave it half done and it picks up where you stopped.
- **Practice** gives endless gradients. Pick Easy or Hard: Hard is a bigger board with only the four corners locked.
- When you finish, **Copy result** copies your time, moves and a link that opens the same gradient for a friend.

## Tip

Start from the locked tiles. Each row and each column fades evenly, so place the edges first and fill the middle last.
