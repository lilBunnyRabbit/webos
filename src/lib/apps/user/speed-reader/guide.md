## Getting text in

- Paste or type text and press **Read** (or Ctrl+Enter).
- Or open a .txt or .md file with the folder button. Files remember where you stopped, so reading picks up there next time.

## Reading

- Click or tap the word, or press Space, to play and pause.
- The minus and plus buttons, or the up and down arrows, change the speed by 50 words a minute (50 to 1200).
- Left and right arrows step one word; with Shift, ten. Home and End jump to the start and end. Drag the bar to scrub.

## Settings

The sliders button sets how many faded words show on each side, and whether the reader holds a word a little longer before punctuation. **Sentence pause** is the extra time at the end of a sentence.

Reading pauses when the window is minimised or hidden.
