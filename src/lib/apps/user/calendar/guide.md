## Getting around

- **Today**, the arrows and the title move through months or weeks; click or tap the title to jump to any date.
- Switch between **Month**, **Week** and **Agenda** in the toolbar. Agenda lists the next 30 days.
- In a narrow window the month grid shows the selected day's events below it.

## Adding events

- **New event** adds one on the selected day. Double-click a day in the month, or an hour in the week, to start one right there.
- Pick a time or **All day**, how it repeats (and until when), a reminder, a colour and notes.
- Click or tap an event to edit it. For a repeating event, **Delete this one** removes just that day.

## Reminders

Reminders ring while WebOS is open in any tab, even with Calendar closed. Clicking the notification opens the event's day.

## More

The **More** menu (⋯) shows or hides worked hours from Work Hours and due dates from Kanban, and adds a Today widget to the desktop (desktop only). In the Terminal, try `cal`, `cal agenda` or `cal add Dentist --day tomorrow --at 14:30 --remind 1h`.
