## How a set works

- By default you focus for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute short break. After 4 focus sessions comes a 15-minute long break. The dots under the time show where you are in the set.
- Change the lengths, and whether breaks or the next focus start on their own, in **Settings**. Changes apply from the next phase.
- Skipping a focus session doesn't count it. Only finished ones go into **Stats**.

## It keeps time without the window

- The timer runs on with Pomodoro closed, as long as WebOS is open in a tab. Turn on **Sound** for a chime, and allow **Browser notifications** to see phase ends from another tab.
- **Add to desktop** puts a small timer with start and pause on your desktop (not on phones).
- In the Terminal: `pomo start`, `pomo pause`, `pomo skip`, `pomo status`, `pomo stats`.
