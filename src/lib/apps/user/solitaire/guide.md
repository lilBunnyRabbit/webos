## The goal

Move all 52 cards onto the four piles at the top, one per suit, from ace up to king.

## The rules

- In the seven columns, build down in alternating colours: a red 9 on a black 10.
- You can move a run of face-up cards together. Only a king, or a run starting with one, goes on an empty column.
- Tap the stock to draw one card or three, depending on **Draw 1** or **Draw 3**. When it's empty, tap it to turn the waste over.
- Once every card is face up, the game finishes itself.

## Controls

- **Drag** a card or a run to where it goes, or **click or tap** it to send it to the best place.
- Undo with Ctrl+Z. F2 deals a new game.
- **Keyboard:** Left and Right pick a pile, Up and Down pick how many cards, Enter moves them to the best place. Space picks up and drops, Escape puts back, D draws.

## Score

Points come from moving cards to the top piles, from the waste to a column, and turning cards over. Moving a card back down from the top costs points, and so does turning the waste over in Draw 1. A fast win earns a time bonus.

## Daily

The daily deal is Draw 1, the same for everyone, and Game Hub keeps your results.
