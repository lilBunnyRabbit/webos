## A day at the shop

- **Warm-up:** press the key that lights up, all ten digits. A wrong key costs nothing.
- **Customers:** type each price on the list and press Enter, then type the total and press Enter. You only see if it was right at the end: the register says how much over or short you were and marks the wrong lines.
- A customer who doesn't trust you asks for the total twice.
- There's no clock. Each day has a few more customers than the one before, and from day 3 prices have cents.

## Controls

- **Keyboard:** the top row or the numpad, `.` or `,` for cents, Backspace, Enter. **H** hires staff.
- **Phone:** tap the register's keypad.

## Staff and the police

- **Staff** cost cash and ring up the next four customers' lists for you, so you only type the totals.
- When the police watch, the register goes red and your digits show as stars. One wrong total means a night in jail and the shop shuts for the day. Three nights close the shop for good.

## Closing up

- The day's books show takings, wages, bills and stock. Cash can go below zero.
- **Restock:** type the two-digit code on each sold product's tile to refill it.
- Before the next day you can switch on **Sale tomorrow**: lower prices, more regulars, more trust.
- Stars rate your accuracy for the day.

The shop is saved at the end of each day. Closing the window mid-day loses that day.
