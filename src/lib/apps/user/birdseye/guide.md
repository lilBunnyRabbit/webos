## Step by step

- Open a video with the folder button, drop it in, paste it, or use **Open with** on a video in Files.
- Pause on a frame where the subject is clear. Press **Box** and drag around it, then **Track**. It follows from that frame to the end, at normal speed, so it takes as long as the video.
- Red marks on the timeline show where it lost the subject. **Next lost** jumps there; box it again and track on from that frame.
- Switch to **Locked** to watch the result. Set the zoom and how the edges fill: **Blur**, **Black** or **Colour**.
- **Save** writes a WebM with the sound next to the original (in Videos for a dropped or pasted one), named `…-birdseye.webm`.

**Follow size** also follows the subject getting bigger or smaller. It applies to the next Track.

## Keys

- Space or K plays and pauses.
- Left and Right step one frame; with Shift, one second.
- B starts a box, Enter tracks it, Escape cancels.
- N goes to the next lost frame.

## Tips

Tracking works best against a plain background like sky. A subject that passes behind something, or in front of trees, may get lost: box it again there.
