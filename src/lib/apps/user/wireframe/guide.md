Every line runs along X (across), Y (into the depth) or Z (up), at the length you type. The drawing is to scale and you can turn it at any time.

## Draw

- **Draw** (D): drag from a point along an axis; the axis nearest your drag lights up and a length is suggested. Type the length and press **Enter**. The first line starts at the origin.
- Or type a length and press **X**, **Y** or **Z** (with **Shift** for the other way), or tap a spoke on the ring.
- Each line starts where the last one ended. Tap another point to draw from there. A line that ends on an existing point joins it.
- **Slope** (L): tap two points to join them with a line in any direction.
- **Point** (P): tap a point and type an X, Y, Z offset for a new point, for a part that touches nothing.
- **Box** (B): tap a corner and type width × depth × height.

## Change

- **Select** (V): click a line or a point; Shift adds to the selection, Ctrl+A selects every line. On a phone, tapping more lines adds them.
- **Move** (G): tap the corner of the selection to hold it by, then tap the point to drop it on. Esc cancels. **M**, or **By length** while moving, moves it by a typed length along an axis instead. A line whose ends no longer line up becomes a slope.
- **Duplicate** (Ctrl+D) drops a copy the same way. **Copy** (Ctrl+C) puts the selection on the clipboard and **Paste** (Ctrl+V) drops it, held by its lowest corner, in this drawing or another Wireframe window.
- **Extrude** (E) copies the selected lines along an axis and joins each copy to its original. **Copy along** (C) repeats them a step apart as many times as you set.
- **Fill** (F): select lines that close one flat loop to fill it. A face hides what's behind it; tap inside it to select it.
- The colour button sets the colour of the next lines and recolours the selected lines and faces. Delete removes the selection.
- **File › Merge overlapping points** joins points that ended up in the same place.

## Look around

- Right-drag or middle-drag turns the view, Shift with it pans, left-drag on empty space pans, the wheel zooms. On a phone two fingers moved together turn the view, a pinch zooms and pans, one finger pans.
- The **View** menu (or Alt+1 to Alt+5) has Cavalier, Isometric, Front, Side and Top. Ctrl+0 fits the drawing.
- When two axes line up on screen you can't draw along them; turn the view a little.
- **View › All lengths** (Shift+L) hides the lengths except on the selected line and the one under the pointer.

## Saving

The **File** menu saves .wire files (Ctrl+S) and exports the current view as PNG or SVG.
