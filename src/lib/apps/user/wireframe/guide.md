Every line runs along X (across), Y (into the depth) or Z (up), at the length you type. The drawing is to scale and you can turn it at any time.

## Draw

- **Draw** (D): drag from a point along an axis; the axis nearest your drag lights up and a length is suggested. Type the length and press **Enter**. The first line starts at the origin.
- Or type a length and press **X**, **Y** or **Z** (with **Shift** for the other way), or tap a spoke on the ring.
- Each line starts where the last one ended. Tap another point to draw from there. A line that ends on an existing point joins it.
- **Slope** (L): tap two points to join them with a line in any direction.
- **Point** (P): tap a point and type an X, Y, Z offset for a new point, for a part that touches nothing.
- **Box** (B): tap a corner and type width × depth × height.

## Change

- **Select** (V): click a line or a point; Shift adds to the selection, Ctrl+A selects every line.
- **Move** (M) moves the selected points by a length along an axis. A line whose ends no longer line up becomes a slope.
- **Extrude** (E) copies the selected lines along an axis and joins each copy to its original. **Copy** (C) repeats them a step apart as many times as you set.
- The colour button sets the colour of the next lines and recolours the selected ones. Delete removes the selection.
- **File › Merge overlapping points** joins points that ended up in the same place.

## Look around

- Right-drag or middle-drag turns the view, Shift with it pans, left-drag on empty space pans, the wheel zooms. On a phone two fingers turn and pinch, one finger pans.
- The **View** menu (or Alt+1 to Alt+5) has Cavalier, Isometric, Front, Side and Top. Ctrl+0 fits the drawing.
- When two axes line up on screen you can't draw along them; turn the view a little.

## Saving

The **File** menu saves .wire files (Ctrl+S) and exports the current view as PNG or SVG.
