## The goal

Place one rabbit in every row, every column and every meadow (the coloured areas). No two rabbits may touch, not even at a corner.

## Marking cells

- **Click or tap** a cell to cycle it: blank, cross, rabbit, blank. Crosses are your notes for "no rabbit here".
- **Right-click** places or removes a rabbit straight away.
- **Drag** across blank cells to cross them all. A drag that starts on a cross erases crosses instead. A drag never touches rabbits.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move, Space or Enter cycles, X crosses, R places a rabbit, Backspace clears.

## Help along the way

- **Auto-cross** (A, or the crossed-square button) greys out every cell a placed rabbit rules out, so you don't have to cross them yourself.
- A rabbit that breaks a rule turns red, and a row, column or meadow with no place left is marked.
- **Hint** (H) first fixes a wrong mark. Otherwise it makes the next move logic proves, outlines the row, column or meadow it's about, and says why.
- Undo and redo with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z. F2 starts a new puzzle. On a phone, Auto-cross, Undo, Redo and Hint sit in the bar under the board.

## Timer and daily

The clock starts when you press Start and pauses while the window is hidden. A puzzle you leave is kept for next time. Best times per size count only solves without hints; the side panel shows them (the chart button on a narrow window). The daily is an 8×8 puzzle, the same for everyone, and Game Hub keeps your results.
