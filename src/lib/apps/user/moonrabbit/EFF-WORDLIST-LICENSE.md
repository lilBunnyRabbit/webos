# EFF Large Wordlist

`eff-large-wordlist.txt` is the Electronic Frontier Foundation's Large Wordlist for Passphrases (7,776 words, 2016), from https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt (SHA-256 of the downloaded file: `addd35536511597a02fa0a9ff1e5284677b8883b83e986e43f15a3db996b903e`).

Changed: the five-digit dice numbers in front of each word were removed; the words and their order are as published.
