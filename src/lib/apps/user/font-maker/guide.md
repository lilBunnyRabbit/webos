## Drawing letters

- Click or tap a character in the grid to open its box. In a narrow window the arrow at the top goes back to the grid.
- Draw inside the box with a pen, a finger or the mouse. The lines show the baseline, x-height and cap height.
- **Width** sets how thick your lines are. With a mouse or finger, **Even** keeps the line the same all the way, **Speed** makes fast strokes thinner. A pen always uses its real pressure.
- **Undo** takes back the last change (Ctrl+Z, Cmd+Z on a Mac; redo with Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y). On a touch screen, a quick two-finger tap also undoes. **Clear** empties the box.
- `[` and `]`, or the arrows beside the letter, go to the previous and next character. The strip under the box scrolls through every character; click or tap one to jump to it.

## Printing and scanning

- **File › Print template** prints a sheet of empty boxes; **Save template as PNG** saves it as a picture instead.
- Fill it in by hand and photograph it, then use the scan button in the toolbar. Line the four corners up with the black squares on the sheet (the top-left one has a hole), pick which sheet it is when your font has more than one, and press **Use guides**.
- Your writing then shows faintly behind each box. Trace over it with strokes; the eye button hides it. Nothing is traced for you.

## Spacing and kerning

- The **Edit spacing** button shows the box's side edges: drag them, or use − and +, to give a letter more or less room. **Reset to auto** puts it back.
- Type in the sample line to see your font as it grows; letters you haven't drawn show in the system font.
- **Auto kerning** tucks pairs like AV and To together. Click or tap between two letters in the sample (or use the arrow keys there) and use − and + to adjust that pair.

## Weights and italic

- The sliders button opens the font settings: its name, which characters it covers, its weights, an italic, and the guide heights.
- Every weight is made from the same drawing, just thicker or thinner. To redraw a letter for one weight or for the italic, pick that face above the box and draw; **Use regular** (or the face it names) drops the redraw.

## Saving and exporting

- **File › Save** keeps your project as a `.fontmaker` file to come back to (Ctrl+S, Ctrl+O to open).
- **Export** saves the whole family as one `.ttc` file, one face as a `.ttf`, or a variable font that covers every weight. A letter redrawn with a different number of strokes than its regular one can't go into a variable font; Export tells you which.
- **Export › Use as app font** makes your font the UI or code font of WebOS. Change it back in Settings › Text & interface.
