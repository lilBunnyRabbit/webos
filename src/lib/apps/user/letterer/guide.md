## Guessing

- Type a word on your keyboard or the one on screen, then press Enter. Backspace deletes.
- Each tile shows how close you came: **green** is the right letter in the right place, **amber** is in the word but elsewhere, grey is not in the word.
- Guesses must be real words of the right length.

## Modes

- **Classic:** one word, six guesses.
- **Multi:** two, four or eight words at once. Every guess goes on every board, and you get five guesses plus one per board. The small bars on each key show that letter on each board.
- **Survival:** solve a word and the next one starts with your answer as its first row. Each word gets six rows, the carried one included, and the run ends when one beats you.
- **Dodge:** the word keeps changing to avoid your guesses, with no guess limit. Corner it until your guess is the only word left.
- **Math:** guess an 8-character equation using digits and `+ - × ÷ =`. It needs exactly one `=` and both sides must be equal. On a keyboard, type `*` and `/` for × and ÷.

Pick five, six or seven letters in every mode but Math.

## Hard mode

Turn on **Hard** and every hint you've revealed must be used in later guesses. Switch it before your first guess. Multi plays without it.

## Daily and stats

The daily word is Classic with five letters, the same for everyone. Start it from Game Hub, or type `letterer daily` in Terminal (`letterer daily yesterday` plays a past one).

Statistics are kept per mode and length: played, win rate, streaks and wins by guesses. On a narrow window, the chart button opens them. **New game** or F2 starts over; a game in progress stays when you close the window.
