## Adding a card

- **Add › New card**, then **Scan** with the camera, **Scan a photo**, or type the number and pick the format yourself.
- You can also drop or paste a photo of the card anywhere in the window.
- Give it a name and a colour, and add notes or photos of the front and back if you like.

## At the till

Open a card and press **Show at checkout**. The code fills the window at full contrast and the screen stays on. Tap anywhere, or press Escape, to close it.

## Keeping it tidy

- **Pin to the top** keeps a card first. The rest sort by how often you use them.
- **Share as QR** on a card makes a code any phone camera can open, with that one card in it.

## Moving to another device

- **More › Share list as QR** shows your whole list as a few codes in turn. On the other device, **Add › Import from QR** and hold the camera on them until it has them all.
- **More › Export as file…** saves a zip with every card and photo; **Import file…** reads it back.
- Importing never removes a card. You review new and changed cards before they're added.

Cards stay on this device and are part of your backups.
