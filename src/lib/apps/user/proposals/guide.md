## Writing a proposal

- Press **New proposal**, give it a title, then answer the sections you can. Each one shows its question until you write in it, and none is required.
- The bars beside each proposal show how many sections are filled.
- Set the status to Draft, Sent or Shipped. The list can show just one of them.

## Pictures

- Paste or drop a picture into a section, or use the picture button beside the section's name: a photo, a file, or **Sketch…**.
- Click or tap a picture to switch it between small in the sentence and full width. Backspace deletes it like a letter.
- Sketches have a pen, an eraser and Undo, then **Insert**.

## Saving and sending

- The **File** menu (the notebook name on a phone) makes a new notebook, opens one, and saves it (Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Shift+S for Save as). Notebooks are `.proposals.md` files.
- **Send** shares the whole notebook or only the open proposal as one Markdown file. Where your browser can share files it opens the share sheet; otherwise the file downloads. Sending doesn't change your saved notebook.
