## The goal

Every puzzle has a theme and a list of words. Find each one in the grid. Words run in straight lines and can cross each other.

## Finding a word

- **Drag** from the first letter to the last.
- Or **tap** the first letter, then the last.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move, Space or Enter marks the first and last letter, Escape cancels.
- A found word gets its own colour and is crossed off the list.

## Difficulty

- **Easy:** 10×10, 8 words, only across and down.
- **Medium:** 13×13, 12 words, diagonals too.
- **Hard:** 16×16, 16 words, and words can read backwards.

## Hints, timer and daily

**Hint** (H) shows the first letter of a word you haven't found. F2 starts a new puzzle. The clock starts with your first move, and best times count only solves without hints. The daily is a Medium puzzle, the same for everyone, and Game Hub keeps your results.
