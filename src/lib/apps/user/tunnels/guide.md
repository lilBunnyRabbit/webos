## The goal

The board hides 3 to 5 words of 5 to 7 letters. Together they use every letter exactly once. Find them all.

## Rules

- A word runs through letters that touch up, down, left or right. It can bend, but never goes diagonally.
- The word list above the board shows only how long each word is.
- A real word in the wrong place doesn't count: it has to be one of the hidden words, on exactly its letters.

## Tracing a word

- **Drag** through the letters. Drag back to take letters off. Let go to check the word.
- Or **tap** the letters one by one, then tap the last one again to check.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move, Space adds a letter, Enter checks, Backspace drops a letter, Escape clears.
- A found word locks in with its own colour. Anything else shakes and fades.

## Stuck?

- **Hint** (H) shows the next letter of a word, with its place on the board.
- **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) takes back the last word you found, **Reset** takes back all of them. Hinted letters stay shown.
- F2 starts a new puzzle.

## Sizes, timer and daily

Small, Medium and Large change the board and the number of words. The clock starts with your first move. Best times count only solves without hints. The daily is a Medium board, the same for everyone, and Game Hub keeps your results.
