## Getting started

- Open **API key** and follow **Make a key on your ArenaNet account**. Tick the permissions you want; keys can only read.
- Paste the whole key and press **Save**. The app checks it and takes you to **Today**.
- The **Permissions** list shows what each one unlocks. *account* is always needed; *progression* is for Today, *wallet* for Wallet, *inventories* for Inventory and Goals, and *characters* adds each character's bags.
- A view the key can't read says which permission is missing. Make a new key with it and paste that one in.

## The views

- **Today:** Wizard's Vault objectives under **Daily**, **Weekly** and **Special**, and the world bosses you've looted today under **Bosses**.
- **Wallet:** every currency, with a filter box.
- **Inventory:** everything you own in one grid. Search by name or item id, filter by type and rarity, and sort. Click or tap an item to see where each one sits, equipped gear included.
- **Goals:** press **Add goal**, set a **Target**, then pick one of your items or type an item id to track something you don't own yet. Progress counts your bank, materials, shared slots and bags. Change the target on the card, or remove the goal with its bin button.

In a narrow window the views move into a menu at the top left.

## Good to know

- Data comes from the official Guild Wars 2 API. The app reuses what it fetched for up to five minutes; press **Refresh** to fetch again now. The time beside it shows when it last updated.
- The key stays in this browser. **Remove** under **Current key** deletes it.
- In the Terminal, `gw2 daily` lists today's objectives.
