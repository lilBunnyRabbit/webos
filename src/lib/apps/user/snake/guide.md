Eat the food to grow one longer and score a point. The game ends when you hit a wall or your own body. Fill the whole board to win.

- **Steer** with the arrow keys or WASD, swipe on the board, or tap the arrow pad on a touch screen. The first move starts the game.
- **Pause** with Space, P or Escape. The game also pauses when you switch to another window.
- **Wrap** turns the walls off: leave one side and you come back on the other.

Each speed, with walls or with wrap, keeps its own best score. Changing either starts a new game.
