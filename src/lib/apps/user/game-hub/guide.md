## The tabs

- **Library:** every installed game. Click or tap a card to play. Sort by Recent, A–Z or Most played.
- **Today:** today's daily puzzles. **Play** opens a game on today's puzzle, and **Again** replays one you've solved. Your time or score shows once it's done.
- **Stats:** total play time, sessions, games played and dailies solved, then each game's play time.
- **Trophies:** the achievements of every game, and how many you've unlocked.

## Streak

Your streak counts the days in a row you solved at least one daily. It's still alive until midnight if you solved one yesterday. New puzzles arrive at midnight.

## On the desktop

On a computer, **Add to desktop** puts a Game Hub widget on your desktop: your streak, today's puzzles and your play time. Click a puzzle there to play it.

Play time and dailies are counted by the games themselves, so they show up here without any setup.
