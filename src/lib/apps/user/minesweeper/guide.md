## The goal

Open every cell that isn't a mine. A number tells how many of the eight cells around it hold a mine.

## Controls

- **Click or tap** a cell to open it. The first one is never a mine.
- **Right-click** or **long press** to plant a flag, and again to take it off.
- Click a number once all its mines are flagged to open the rest of its neighbours at once. If a flag is wrong, that opens a mine.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move, Home and End jump along the row, Enter or Space opens, F flags. F2 starts a new game, and so does the face button.

The counter on the left is mines minus flags. It goes below zero when you flag too many.

## Boards

- Beginner is 9×9 with 10 mines, Intermediate 16×16 with 40, Expert 30×16 with 99.
- **Custom** takes a width, height and mine count; press **Apply**.
- Statistics keep games played, won and the best time per difficulty. Custom has no best time.

## Daily

The daily is an Intermediate board, the same for everyone, and starts with its middle already open. Play it from Game Hub, or with `minesweeper daily` in the Terminal (`minesweeper daily yesterday` for a past one).
