## Harbour

A small harbour you sail around. Every numbered pair of buoys is a level: sail between them and stop, and its card opens. **Random** and **Daily** are the two gates on the right. Nothing is judged in the harbour itself: bump into what you like.

- The **boat shed**, top left, swaps your boat: sail in with the RIB and come out in the yacht, or back.
- The board by each gate shows the level's number and your stars in the boat you're sailing.

## The helm

- **The lever** does gear and throttle in one: Full, Half and Slow astern, **N**eutral, Slow, Half and Full ahead. It stays where you leave it. **W** and **S** (or **↑** **↓**) move it a notch, **Space** puts it in neutral.
- From ahead to astern the gearbox pauses in neutral for a moment, and the engine takes a moment to answer. Plan ahead.
- **The wheel** turns with **A** and **D** (or **←** **→**) and stays where you leave it. **C** centres it.
- Rather hold a key? Settings › **Throttle › Hold**: hold W for half ahead, S for half astern, let go for neutral.
- On a touch screen: the wheel under your left thumb, the lever under your right. Drag the lever to a notch.
- Pinch, the scroll wheel or **+** **−** zoom.

## The boats

- **RIB**, 4.5 m, with an outboard. The whole motor turns, so it steers going ahead and astern, even from a standstill. Light, it stops quickly and slides in a turn.
- **Yacht**, 10 m, one propeller and a rudder behind it. The rudder only works with water flowing past it: a short burst of ahead with the wheel over ("a kick") swings the stern even when you're barely moving. Astern the rudder is weak and the propeller walks the stern to port. It carries its way: about 18 m from walking pace to a stop.

## Docking

- The berth is the dashed outline. **Alongside**: lie along the pontoon, either way round, close to its edge. **Box berth**: bow in between the fingers.
- Stop inside it, square to it (within 8°), and hold for a second. The crew step off and put the lines on.
- Touching is fine, if it's gentle. A touch is judged by how fast you close on it: under 0.3 kn is a **kiss**, under 1 kn a **bump**, faster ends the round. A quay wall or someone else's boat has no fenders and takes half of each.

## Stars

- ★ docked, ★ gentle (every touch a kiss), ★ on time (at or under par). Par comes from an autopilot that docks every level in the same physics, with room to spare.
- 95% precision or better (centred, square, close in) is **Perfect**.
- The **Daily** is the same berth for everyone that day, in the boat the day picks.
