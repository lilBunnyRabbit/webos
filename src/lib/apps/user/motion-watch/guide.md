## Watching

- Press **Arm** and allow the camera. It keeps watching until you press **Disarm**, even with the window closed. Open the app again to see it live.
- When something moves you get a notification, and the clip goes to `/home/Videos/Motion`, one folder per day.
- A light switching on or off, which changes most of the picture at once, doesn't count.
- You can also stop it in Activity Monitor › Background, or with `bg stop motion-watch` in the Terminal.
- If WebOS reloads while it's armed, it asks whether to resume.

## Tuning

- **Sensitivity:** higher catches smaller changes. Lower it if it keeps firing on nothing. Changes apply while it watches.
- **Zones** lays a grid over the picture. Drag across cells to switch them off or on; ignored cells are dark. Press **Done** when finished, or **Watch all** to start over.
- **Keep clips for** deletes days older than that the next time you arm.

## Events

Pick a day. Each mark on the 24-hour strip, and each row in the list, plays that clip; the percentage is how much of the watched picture moved. **Live** goes back to the camera.

## Limits

- Needs Chrome or Edge.
- Video only, no sound, from the default camera.
- A phone stops the camera when the screen turns off or the browser goes to the background. Leave it open on a charger and tap **Dim** to darken the screen; tap again to wake it.
- Delete clips in File Explorer.
