## Recording

- Pick **Mic**, **Screen** or **Camera** at the top, then press the big button. Pause and resume as often as you like, then **Stop**.
- **Screen** asks the browser what to share: a tab, a window or the whole screen. **Include microphone** adds your voice. Ending the share in the browser stops the recording too. Screen recording isn't available on phones.
- **Camera** can **Take photo** or **Record video**. Pick JPEG or PNG for photos, and switch cameras when the device has more than one.

## Keeping it

- After you stop, play the take back first. **Save** keeps it, **Discard** throws it away.
- Everything is saved in `/home/Recordings`. **Recent** lists it; click or tap one to open it.
