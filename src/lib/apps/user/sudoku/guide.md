## The goal

Fill every blank cell so each row, column and box holds every digit once: 1 to 9 on 9×9, 1 to 6 on 6×6. Bold digits are given and can't change. A digit that repeats turns red.

## Playing

- Click or tap a cell, then a digit on the pad. On a keyboard, move with the arrows and type the digit.
- Entering the digit a cell already holds clears it. **Erase** (Backspace, Delete or 0) clears it too.
- The small number under each pad digit is how many are still to place.

## Notes

- Turn **Notes** on (N) and digits go in as small pencil marks instead. Shift with a digit writes a single note either way.
- **Fill notes** (Shift+N) writes every digit each blank cell still allows.
- Placing a digit removes it from the notes in its row, column and box.

## Stuck?

- **Hint** (H) first fixes a wrong digit. Otherwise it places the next digit logic proves and names the technique.
- Undo with Ctrl+Z, redo with Ctrl+Shift+Z. F2 starts a new puzzle.
- The clock starts with your first move. Best times count only solves without hints. Your puzzle is kept when you close the window.
