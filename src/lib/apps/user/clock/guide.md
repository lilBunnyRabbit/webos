## Tabs

- **All** shows everything at once: timers, the stopwatch, alarms and your cities. Clock opens on it when two or more things are running.
- **World** lists your cities next to the local time. **Add city** searches places and time zones; Enter adds the first match.
- **Alarms**: **New alarm** sets a time, the days it repeats (none means once) and a label. Click or tap an alarm to edit or delete it; the switch turns it on or off.
- **Timer**: pick a preset or type hours, minutes and seconds, name it if you like, then **Start**. Several can run at once.
- **Stopwatch** keeps laps, each with its split and total.

## When it rings

Alarms and timers ring while WebOS is open in any tab, even with Clock closed. Allow notifications when the browser asks, and you also get a system notification with **Snooze 10 min** and **Stop**.

## Extras

- **Add to desktop** (World tab, desktop only) pins an analog or digital clock to the desktop.
- In the Terminal: `time Tokyo`, `alarm add 07:30 -d weekdays`, `timer 5m tea`, `alarm ls`, `timer ls`.
