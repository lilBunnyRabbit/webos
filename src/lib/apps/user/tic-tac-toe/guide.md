Get three of your marks in a row, across, down or diagonally. A full board with no line is a draw.

- **Modes:** Easy, Medium and Impossible play the computer. You are always X and move first. 2 players passes one device back and forth.
- **Playing:** click or tap a cell. On a keyboard, press 1–9 (top left to bottom right), or move with the arrows and press Enter.
- **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) takes back your last move, and the computer's reply with it. Changing the mode starts a new game.
- **Statistics** keep wins, losses and draws per mode. Impossible never loses, so a draw there is the best you can do.
