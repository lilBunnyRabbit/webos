## Lists

The first screen shows every list and **All lists**. Tap one to open it, the gear for its name, colour and icon, or **New list** to start another.

## Adding items

- Type in the bar at the bottom and press Enter. An amount at the front or end comes off: `2x milk`, `500 g flour`, `eggs x10`.
- Adding something already on the list updates its amount instead of adding it twice.
- After a trip, chips above the field offer what you bought last time.

## Ticking off

- **Swipe right** on an item to put it in the cart, swipe right again to take it out. **Swipe left** all the way to delete, with Undo.
- With a mouse, click the checkbox. Tap or click the item itself to change its name or amount.
- Long-press an item (or right-click it, or use its ⋯) to move it to another list.
- **Clear done** empties the cart and remembers the trip for suggestions and Buy again.

## In the store

The storefront button at the top turns on in-store mode: big rows, and the screen stays on.

## From a photo

The camera button reads a handwritten or printed list from a photo or a scan. You check every line before anything is added. The first read may download about 17 MB; after that it works offline.

## Sharing with another phone

In the ⋯ menu, **Share…** shows your lists as QR codes. On the other phone, **Receive…** scans them and lists every change; untick any you don't want, then apply.

Lists stay on this device. Add one to the desktop or home screen from its ⋯ menu.
