## A round

- Press **Start**. You see a word and the five words you can't say.
- Type a clue and press Enter or **Send**. The AI makes up to three guesses after each clue.
- Keep adding clues until it guesses the word, then go to the **Next card**. After five cards, **See the score**.
- Can't get it there? **Pass** shows the word and moves on.

## Clues

- Saying the word, a banned word, or a form of one (plurals, -ing, -ed and so on) stops the clue. The words that broke the rule are marked under the field.
- The AI remembers every clue for the current card, so build on what you already said. It won't repeat a wrong guess.
- Taking too long? **Stop** (or Escape) cancels the guess, and your clue stays in the field.

## Score

Lower is better. Each clue costs one point and a pass costs three. Your best round is kept. At the end you can copy a link that deals the same five cards to someone else.

The AI runs inside Chrome on a computer, so it doesn't work on a phone. A round isn't saved, so closing the window ends it.
