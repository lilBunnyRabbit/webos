## Airfield

A small airfield seen from the side, at real scale: the hangar and the apron on the left, a 2.4 km runway, grass and trees past both ends. Taxi out, take off, fly on and land back on the runway. The world is a loop: keep flying the way you face and you come back to the field from behind.

- Taxi into the **hangar** to pick a plane: the trainer, the bush plane, the airliner or the glider. **Taxi out** (or Enter) puts the card away.
- Along the apron, left of the hangar, stand seven **gates**: the five levels of the plane you're flying, a **random** landing and the **daily** (the same for everyone, and its plane is picked for you). Stop at one to open its card.
- The little profile top right shows the field or the level ahead, the glidepath and where you are. In the air the **Runway** chip counts down to the runway ahead.

## Flying

- **Lever**: **Shift** (or **E**) up a notch, **Ctrl** (or **Q**) down. The engine takes a moment to answer, the airliner's several seconds.
- **Pitch**: **W** and **S** (or **↑** **↓**) move the stick, gradually: a tap is a nudge, holding pulls harder the longer you hold. Let go and it springs back to the trim. Or drag up and down on the board with the mouse.
- **T** trims: the plane then holds the speed it has now, hands off.
- **F** lowers the flaps a notch, **V** raises them. Flaps lift more at low speed and drag more.
- **B** brakes, harder the longer you hold it. Hard brakes at speed tip a taildragger onto its nose, so keep the stick back.
- **A** and **D** (or **←** **→**) turn round on the ground, below taxi speed. In the air the plane flies the way it faces.
- Settings › **Pitch › Up is up** makes W raise the nose instead.
- On a touch screen: the lever on the left, the pitch pad on the right (drag down to pull the nose up), flaps, brakes, turn and trim between them.
- **Esc** pauses, **R** puts you back on the apron (in a level: restarts), the scroll wheel, a pinch or **+** **−** zoom.

## The planes

- **Trainer**: a light taildragger. Stalls near 35 kn, lifts off by itself near 45 kn, floats.
- **Bush plane**: big flaps and tyres. With flaps 2 it leaps off in about 70 m; with Full flaps it flies at 25 kn and stops in 40 m.
- **Airliner**: 70 t, two jets. Below **Idle** the lever opens the **Spoilers**, and below that **Reverse** (on the ground only). Pull gently near 145 kn to rotate: it won't do it by itself, and too far scrapes the tail. Land near 150 kn; after touchdown the autobrake comes on until you push the power up.
- **Glider**: no engine, 35 m forward for every metre down. The lever works the **airbrakes**. Hold **A** or **D** (◀ ▶ on touch) and the ground crew walk it; on the runway press **L** (Winch launch) and a winch tows it up to about 450 m. Fly slowly through rising air (the **Lift** chip, a cumulus overhead) to climb.

## Weather

- The **windsock** and the **wind chip** show the wind; clouds drift with it. Land into it when you can: a headwind slows you over the ground.
- Gusts shake you on final, **windshear** takes the headwind away near the ground, and wind blowing up a hill rises with it (and sinks behind it).

## Levels and stars

- A level starts paused on its card: **Fly** (or Enter, or any control) starts the clock.
- **Done**: stopped on the strip, or for a takeoff climbed past the flag. **Gentle**: one touchdown under 0.6 m/s, no bounce (a takeoff: never hearing the stall horn). **Under par**: the time is from an autopilot that flies every level.
- Touchdowns: under 0.6 m/s a **kiss**, under 1.5 **firm**, under 3 **heavy**; 3 m/s or more breaks the gear. A stall between 2 and 20 m up, a tree, a mast or the water end the round.
