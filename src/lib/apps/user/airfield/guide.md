## Airfield

A small airfield seen from the side, at real scale: the hangar and the apron on the left, a 650 m runway, grass and trees past both ends. Taxi out, take off, fly wherever you like and land back on the runway. Nothing is scored yet; every touchdown is graded.

- Taxi into the **hangar** to pick a plane. For now it's the trainer, a light taildragger.
- The little profile top right shows the whole field from 3 km out each side, the 3° glidepaths to both ends and where you are.

## Flying

- **Throttle**: **Shift** (or **E**) opens it a notch, **Ctrl** (or **Q**) closes one: Idle, Quarter, Half, Cruise, Full. The engine takes a moment to answer.
- **Pitch**: **W** and **S** (or **↑** **↓**) move the stick; let go and it springs back to the trim. A tap is a nudge, a held key goes all the way. Or drag up and down on the board with the mouse.
- **T** trims: the plane then holds the speed it has now, hands off.
- **F** lowers the flaps a notch (Half, Full), **V** raises them. Flaps lift more at low speed and drag more: Half for takeoff, Full for landing.
- **B** brakes, harder the longer you hold it. Hard brakes at speed tip a taildragger onto its nose, so keep the stick back.
- **A** and **D** (or **←** **→**) turn round to face left or right: on the ground you pivot when slow, in the air the plane banks into a turn and comes round in about 15 seconds. A banked wing loses lift, so hold a little back stick.
- Settings › **Pitch › Pilot** makes up and W push the stick forward, as in a cockpit.
- On a touch screen: the throttle lever on the left, the pitch pad on the right (drag up to raise the nose; it springs back when you let go), flaps, brakes, turn and trim between them.
- **Esc** pauses, **R** puts you back on the apron, the scroll wheel, a pinch or **+** **−** zoom.

## The trainer

- Stalls at about 35 kn with the flaps up, 31 kn with them down. Near the stall the horn sounds and **Stall** flashes; pull further and the wing lets go, the nose drops and you lose height until it flies again.
- Full throttle, stick and trim alone, it lifts the tail and flies off by itself at about 45 kn, after some 150 m with Half flaps.
- Climbs about 3 m/s at 50 kn, cruises at 66 kn, glides 8 m forward for every metre down with the engine at idle.

## Landing

- Come down the glidepath at about 45 kn with Full flaps and the throttle back. Just above the runway, ease the nose up to stop the sink (the flare) and let the plane float: the wing works better close to the ground. It settles on as the speed bleeds off.
- Touchdowns are graded by how fast the wheels meet the runway: under 0.6 m/s a **kiss**, under 1.5 m/s **firm**, under 3 m/s **heavy**. 3 m/s or more breaks the gear.
