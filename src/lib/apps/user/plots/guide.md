## The goal

Split the whole garden into rectangular plots. Every cell belongs to exactly one plot, and every plot holds exactly one clue.

## Reading a clue

- The **number** is the plot's size in cells: a 6 can be 2×3, 3×2, 1×6 or 6×1.
- The **icon** is the plot's shape: a square, a tall rectangle (taller than wide) or a wide one (wider than tall).
- A clue with only a number can be any shape. A clue with only an icon can be any size.

## Drawing plots

- **Drag** from one corner to the opposite one. The outline turns red when the plot breaks a rule, and the status line says why.
- A new plot replaces any plot it overlaps.
- **Tap** a plot (or right-click it) to remove it.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move, Space marks a corner, arrows stretch the plot, Space again places it. Escape cancels, Backspace removes.

## When you're stuck

- **Hint** (H) first removes a plot that's wrong, then places the next plot logic proves, and says why.
- Undo and redo with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z. F2 starts a new puzzle.
- Every puzzle has one answer, and you can always reach it by logic, without guessing.

## Timer and daily

The clock starts when you press Start. Best times count only solves without hints. The daily is a 7×7 puzzle, the same for everyone, and Game Hub keeps your results.
