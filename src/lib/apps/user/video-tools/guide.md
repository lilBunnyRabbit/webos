## The basics

- Open a video with the folder button, drop it on the window, or paste it. In Files, **Open with** on a video lists Video Tools too.
- Pick the part you want: drag the two handles under the video, or play to a spot and press **In** or **Out**.
- Choose **Clip**, **GIF** or **Sound** under **Save as**, then press **Save clip**, **Save GIF** or **Save sound**.

## Saving

- **Clip** saves a WebM file. Saving plays the part through once, so it takes about as long as the part.
- **GIF** shows its frame count and an estimated size before you save. Fewer frames per second or a smaller width makes it smaller.
- **Sound** saves only the audio. It needs a video that has sound.
- **Skip pauses** (Clip and GIF) leaves out the stretches where nothing on screen changes, so a screen recording or a timelapse plays without them. The saved file shows how long the part was and how long the result is.
- **Sensitivity** sets how small a change still counts. Lower it when a blinking cursor or a clock keeps pauses in; raise it when faint changes go missing. It's remembered.
- Skipping pauses removes the sound, and the GIF's frame count and size become an upper limit.
- The new file lands next to the original, or in Videos when the video came from outside WebOS. Open it or show it in its folder from the panel.

## Keys

- Space or K plays and pauses.
- Left and Right step one frame. Hold Shift to jump a second.
- I and O set the in and out points. [ and ] jump to them.
