## The goal

Fill every empty cell so each row and each column holds every digit from 1 up to the grid size once. The signs between cells must hold too: each one points at the smaller of its two digits.

Bold digits are given and can't change. Every puzzle has one answer, reachable by logic alone.

## Filling the grid

- Click or tap a cell, then pick a digit on the pad. Pick the same digit again to clear it, or use **Erase**.
- Keyboard: arrows move, Home and End jump to the ends of the row, digits fill, Backspace, Delete, Space or 0 clears.
- Cells that repeat a digit or break a sign turn red. A sign fades once it's met and turns red when broken.

## Notes

- **Notes** (or Enter, or N) switches the pad to pencil marks: small candidate digits in a cell.
- Shift with a digit notes it without switching. Right-click or long-press a pad digit does the same.
- **M** pencils in every candidate the digits and signs around each cell still allow.

## When you're stuck

- **Hint** (H) first fixes a wrong digit if there is one, otherwise fills the next cell logic proves. The cell flashes.
- Undo and redo with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z. F2 starts a new puzzle.

## Sizes, timer and daily

Pick 4×4 up to 7×7. The clock starts on your first move, and your puzzle is kept when you close the window. Best times count only solves without hints. The daily is a 5×5 puzzle, the same for everyone, and Game Hub keeps your results.
