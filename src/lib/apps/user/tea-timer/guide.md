## Brewing

- Heat the water to the temperature on the tea's card.
- Pick **Light**, **Medium** or **Strong**. Light uses the shortest time, Strong the longest. The tea remembers your pick.
- Press **Brew**. The cup moves to *Your cups* with a countdown, and rings when it's ready, even with this window closed.
- When it's ready, **Steep 2** starts the next steep of the same leaves, a little longer. **Done** clears the cup. The ✕ stops a cup early.

## Your teas

- **Add tea** sets the name, water temperature, shortest and longest time, how many steeps, and notes.
- The **⋯** button on a card edits it. Built-in teas can be hidden or reset to default; your own can be deleted.
- Switch between °C and °F at the top.

Cups ring only while WebOS is open in a browser tab. Several cups can brew at once.
