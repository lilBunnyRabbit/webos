## Your recipes

- Recipes are `.cook` files in `/home/Documents/Recipes`. The folder button on the toolbar picks another folder.
- **New** asks for a name and opens the recipe in Edit with a short example to replace.
- The device button imports `.cook` files you already have. Search by title, tag or ingredient, or tap a tag to filter.

## Writing a recipe

- Each paragraph is one step. A blank line starts the next one.
- Mark things inside a step: `@flour{200%g}` for an ingredient, `#pot{}` for cookware, `~{10%minutes}` for a timer.
- `@salt{=1%tsp}` keeps that amount the same at any number of servings.
- A line starting with `>` is a note, `--` is a comment that doesn't show.
- On a wide window, Edit shows the source and preview side by side. Narrow, switch between **Source** and **Preview**. Save with Ctrl+S.

## Reading and cooking

- **Recipe**, **Table** and **Cook** are the views. The minus and plus buttons change the servings, and **As written**, **Metric** or **Imperial** convert the amounts.
- **Cook** shows one step at a time. Use **Back** and **Next**, or the Left and Right arrow keys.
- Tap a timer in a step to start it. Timers keep running after you close the window and notify you when they're done.
