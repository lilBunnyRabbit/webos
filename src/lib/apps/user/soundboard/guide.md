## Adding sounds

- **Add sounds…** picks audio or video files. You can also drop files on the window or paste them with Ctrl+V.
- Each sound is copied into the board, so moving or deleting the original doesn't break the pad.
- A video opens a trim sheet: drag the handles to pick the part to keep, and the picture follows. Only that part's sound is kept.

## Playing

- Click or tap a pad to play it. A pad with a key plays when you press that key.
- **Esc** or the stop button stops every sound.
- **Restart** plays a pressed pad from the start again; **Overlap** layers a new copy on top.
- A looping pad plays until you press it again.

## Editing a pad

Right-click a pad, long-press it on a phone, or use its ⋯ button. **Edit…** sets the label, colour, picture, volume, key and loop. **Edit audio** in there trims the start and end, cuts out a selection with **Delete**, or keeps only the selection with **Keep**.

Drag pads to reorder them, or use **Move earlier** and **Move later** in the menu.

## Boards

The ⋯ button next to the board name makes a new board, renames it or deletes it. Keys belong to one board, so each board can use the same keys.

In the Terminal, `sb play` plays a pad by name or key, and `sb stop` stops everything.
