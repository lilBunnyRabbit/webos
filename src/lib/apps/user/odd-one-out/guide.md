## Playing

- Press **Start**, then click or tap the tile that looks different. Every find brings a new board at once, from 2×2 up to 9×9.
- A wrong tile shakes and gets a red outline.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move between tiles, Enter picks.
- The clock stops while the window is hidden. A daily you leave halfway keeps its place: press **Resume** to go on.

## Daily, practice and challenges

- **Daily** is one run a day, and Game Hub keeps its score.
- **Practice** plays fresh boards that don't count; **New puzzle** starts another.
- After a run, **Copy result** copies your score with a link. Whoever opens it plays the same boards, as a practice run.
