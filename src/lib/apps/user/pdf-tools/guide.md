Pick a tool from the side list (or the menu at the top in a narrow window). Every tool opens a PDF with **Open…**, or you can drop files onto it. Nothing leaves your device.

## Organize

- Drag pages to reorder them. On a touchscreen, drag by the handle on each page.
- Click or tap a page to select it, then rotate, delete, or insert a blank page from the toolbar.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move between pages, Alt+arrows move the page, **R** rotates (Shift+R the other way), **B** inserts a blank page, Delete removes.
- **Save** writes over the file; **Save as…** keeps the original. A PDF dropped in from your device always saves as a new file.

## Merge, split and images

- **Merge:** add two or more PDFs, put them in order, then **Merge…**. Alt+Up and Alt+Down move the focused file.
- **Split:** choose **Ranges** (like `1-3, 5`), **Extract**, **Every page** or **Every N**. The parts go into a folder you pick.
- **Images to PDF:** each image becomes a page, with the page size and margin you pick.
- **Export images:** pages as PNG or JPEG, at the resolution you pick, into a folder.

## Signing

- Draw a signature in **Signatures** and save it, or press **Draw…** right in **Sign**. Saved signatures live in Signatures in your home folder.
- In **Sign**, click or tap the page to place it, drag to move it, drag the dot to resize. With it selected, arrows nudge, **+** and **-** resize, Delete removes.
- **Save copy…** always writes a new file. It stamps a picture of your signature; it isn't a cryptographic signature.

## Test PDF

Makes sample PDFs with numbered, ruled pages, with a live preview of the first pages. **Exact** builds a file of a given size, like `100MB`.

The Terminal has the same tools: `pdf merge`, `pdf split`, `pdf export` and more.
