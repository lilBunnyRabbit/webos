Pick a tool from the list on the side (on top in a narrow window). Every result uses your browser's secure random source, the same one used for encryption.

- **Number**: whole numbers between two bounds, both included. Turn on **No repeats** to draw each number once at most.
- **Draws**: save a preset with one or more lists, like a lottery ticket, and draw it again any time. **Draw all** draws every preset at once.
- **Dice**: type dice notation such as `3d6+2`, `d20-1` or `2d8+1d4`, or tap a preset. `d%` is a d100.
- **Coin**: flip once or ten at a time. The tally is kept until you reset it.
- **List**: one entry per line. Pick one, pick some, shuffle them or split them into teams.
- **Password**: changing any option makes a new one. Passwords are never saved; copy the one you want.

Your settings and presets are remembered. Dice history and draw results stay with the window.
