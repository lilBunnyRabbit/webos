## Writing a form

Write in **Build**: the text on the left, a live preview on the right (below it in a narrow window).

- The first `# ` heading is the form's title.
- `- [ ]` is a tick box, `- [_]` a short text answer, `- [...]` a long one.
- Add ` | text` at the end of a line for a note under a tick box, or a placeholder in a text box.
- Any other line shows as plain text, so headings and paragraphs split the form into parts.

**Example** loads a sample form. **AI prompt** copies a prompt you can give any AI chat, with what the form is for, to get a form back in this format.

Forms are `.form.md` files. Save with Ctrl+S, save under a new name with Ctrl+Shift+S, open one with Ctrl+O.

## Sharing it

**Share** saves the form and shows its link. The whole form travels inside the link, so very long forms make long links that some chat apps cut off.

Whoever opens the link fills the form in, adds their name if they like, and presses **Create answer code**. Nothing is sent anywhere: they send the code back to you themselves.

## Collecting answers

Paste an answer code into the box on the start screen or in **Answers**, or just paste it anywhere in the window. Forms finds the form it belongs to in your home folder and saves the answer next to it, in a `.answers` file with the same name.

In **Answers**, see a **Summary** per question, **Each** answer on its own, or **Compare** them side by side. **CSV** exports them for a spreadsheet. **Fill** shows the form as people see it.
