## The goal

The suspect gives six statements. Exactly one of them is a lie, and exactly one evidence card proves it. Find that pair.

## Questioning

- The statements are vague on purpose. Ask about them in your own words to get the details: times, people, places, how they got home.
- Enter asks, Shift+Enter adds a line. **Stop** or Escape cuts an answer short; an answer stopped after it began still counts as a question.
- The suspect only knows their own story, never the evidence. Asking which statement is false gets you nowhere.

## Accusing

- Click or tap a statement, then the card that breaks it, then **Accuse**. Click either one again to unpick it.
- You get two tries. A statement from a wrong pair is marked **Tried**.
- When the case closes, the lie and its card are marked, with what the suspect claimed next to the truth. **Transcript** lets you read the questioning again.

## Scoring

Your score is the questions you asked plus your wrong accusations. Lower is better, and a case solved without a single question is possible.

**New case** (F2) starts another. A case stays saved until you start a new one, so you can close the window and come back.
