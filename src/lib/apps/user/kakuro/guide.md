## The goal

Fill every white cell with a digit from 1 to 9. A run is a line of white cells across or down. Each run adds up to its clue, and no digit appears twice in one run.

## Reading a clue

- A dark cell with a diagonal holds the clues for the runs next to it.
- The number in the **top right** is the sum of the run going right. The number in the **bottom left** is the sum of the run going down.
- A clue fades once its run is complete and correct, and turns red when the run can no longer add up.

## Entering digits

- Click or tap a cell, then press a digit key or use the number pad below the board.
- **Notes:** switch to Notes (N, or the pencil button) to pencil in candidates. On a keyboard, Shift with a digit uses the other mode for that one key.
- Backspace, Delete or 0 clears a digit, then the cell's notes. Placing a digit removes it from the notes in its runs.
- Arrow keys move between white cells, skipping clue cells. Home and End jump to the ends of the row.

## Help while solving

- Under the board, both runs of the selected cell show their sum, length and the digit sets that still fit, like `8+9`.
- On the number pad, digits already used in the selected cell's runs are dimmed.
- **Hint** (H) first corrects a wrong digit, otherwise fills the next cell logic proves.
- Undo with Ctrl+Z, redo with Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y. F2 starts a new puzzle.

## Sizes, timer and daily

Pick 6×6 up to 12×12 in the toolbar. The clock starts on your first move. Best times per size count only solves without hints; the Statistics panel shows them. The daily is an 8×8 puzzle, the same for everyone, opened from Game Hub, which keeps your results. Your puzzle in progress is kept when you close the window.
