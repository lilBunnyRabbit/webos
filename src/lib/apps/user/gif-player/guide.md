Open a GIF or a video with **Open a file…**, by dropping it on the window, or by pasting it. In File Explorer, it's under Open with for GIFs and videos.

- Space plays and pauses. Left and right arrows step one frame, Home and End jump to the first and last.
- Drag the slider to scrub. The thin bar under it fills as the GIF's frames are decoded.
- Pick a speed from 0.1× to 4× in the toolbar.
- The status bar shows the size, the frame count and the current frame's delay.

Videos play without sound. They don't say how many frames they have, so a step is 1/30 of a second. A copied image is often turned into a still PNG, so open the `.gif` file itself.
