## The rules

- Each number is an island of exactly that many cells, and every island holds exactly one number. Islands never touch side by side.
- Every other cell is sea, and all the sea joins up into one, through sides, not corners.
- No 2×2 block may be all sea.

An island in red breaks a rule. Every puzzle has exactly one answer.

## Controls

- **Click** a cell to mark it with your tool, **Sea** or **Dot**. Click again for the other mark, and once more to clear it. Right-click or Shift-click goes the other way.
- A dot marks a cell you know is land.
- **Tap** puts down the tool's mark, and tapping it again clears it. **Hold** a finger for the other mark.
- **Drag** to paint a straight line.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move, Space or Enter steps through the marks, S sets sea, D or . sets a dot, Backspace clears. Hold Shift with the arrows to paint with your tool as you go.
- Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes. F2 starts a new puzzle.

**Hint** (H) first fixes a cell you got wrong, then marks one more cell. Best times count only solves without hints.

## Sizes and daily

- Sizes go from 5×5 to 12×12. The puzzle in progress is kept for next time.
- The daily is a 7×7 puzzle, the same for everyone, kept apart from your regular game. Play it from Game Hub, or with `nurikabe daily` in the Terminal.
