## Taking photos

- Glimpse opens on the camera when you have no photos, and on your photos otherwise. The big camera button opens the camera.
- Tap the round shutter, or press Space on a keyboard. Every photo taken while the camera is open belongs to one batch.
- The chip at the top sets when the batch erases itself: **1 h**, **24 h**, **3 d** or **1 w**. You can change it until you close the camera.
- When the camera allows it you also get the torch (off, one-shot flash, or on), zoom (pinch or the slider), tap to focus, and a button to switch cameras.
- The picture in the corner is your last shot. Tap it to see the batch.

## Your photos

- Batches are listed newest first, with the time left before they erase. Tap a photo to see it full size; swipe or use the arrow keys to move through all of them, pinch or scroll to zoom.
- **Save to Pictures** keeps a copy in `/home/Pictures` for good. **Extend** gives the batch a new time from now. **Delete** removes the photo at once.
- The pencil names a batch; the bin deletes the whole batch.

## Limits

- Photos are as sharp as the camera's live picture, not the full sensor.
- Photos erase while Glimpse is open, or the next time you open it.
- They're stored only on this device, in Glimpse's own folder, so they don't show in File Explorer.
