## Typing

- Click or tap the keys, or type: digits, `+ - * /` (or `x`), `%`, brackets, `.` or `,` for the decimal point.
- Enter or `=` gives the result. Backspace deletes, Escape or Delete clears.
- The answer shows below the expression as you type. Unclosed brackets close themselves on =.

## Percent

`200 + 10%` is 220: after `+` or `−`, a percent is a share of what comes before it. Anywhere else `10%` is just 0.1.

## Keeping results

- After =, a digit starts a new sum and an operator carries the result on.
- Ctrl+C or the copy button copies the result.
- History keeps your last 50 results. Click or tap one to use its expression again. In a narrow window, open it with the clock button.
