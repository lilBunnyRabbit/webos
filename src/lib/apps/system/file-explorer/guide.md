## Getting around

- Double-click a folder or file to open it. On a phone, tap.
- **Back** and **Forward** step through the folders you visited, and the path bar jumps to any folder above this one.
- **Filter** narrows the current folder by name.
- In a wide window, a side panel lists your places, the system folders and folders mounted from your disk.

## Selecting

- Click to select, Ctrl+click to add or remove one, Shift+click for a range.
- Drag over empty space to box-select. Ctrl+A selects everything, Escape clears.
- On a phone, tap opens and there's no multi-select.

## Menus and keys

- Right-click a file, or long-press it on a phone, for its menu: Open with, Cut, Copy, Rename, Save to disk, Compress, Delete, Properties, and more for images and zips.
- Right-click empty space, or long-press it, for New folder, Upload, Paste, Reload and Open in Terminal.
- Enter opens, F2 renames, Alt+Enter shows properties, Delete deletes.
- Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V copy, cut and paste. A name clash asks whether to replace, skip or keep both.
- Renaming several items at once lets you use a pattern, like `{name}`, `{n:3}` or `{date:yyyy-MM-dd}`, or find and replace, with a preview before anything changes.

## Moving files

- Drag items onto a folder to move them there, or into another File Explorer window or onto the desktop.
- Drop files or folders from your device into the list to copy them in.

## Where your files live

- Your files are stored in this browser, on this device. **Save to disk** copies a file or folder out to your real disk.
- The system folders (`/etc`, `/var`, `/tmp`) are read-only here: WebOS writes them itself.
- **Upload › Mount a folder** shows a real folder from your disk without copying it, when your browser allows it. Changes there, deletes included, happen on your disk. The browser forgets the access between visits: click the folder to reconnect.
