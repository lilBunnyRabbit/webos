Pick a section from the side list. In a narrow window or on a phone, the list and the page take turns: **Back** at the top of a page returns to the list.

## Windows and widgets

- Each row shows how busy a window or widget is: page changes per second (**Mut/s**), the peak, and how many elements it draws. Wider windows show more columns.
- Click or tap a column header to sort by it, again to flip the order.
- **End** closes a window at once, without asking to save.
- The numbers only update while Activity Monitor is on screen.

## Storage

- **Disk usage** draws your files as boxes sized by the space they take. Click or tap a folder to go inside, a file to see its size. **Reveal in Explorer** opens the spot in File Explorer.
- Backspace or Alt+Up goes up a folder, and so does Back on a phone.

## Tasks and Background

- **Tasks** lists slow jobs apps run, like zipping or exporting. Cancel a running one, retry one that failed, or expand it to see its steps.
- **Background** lists apps running with no window. Open or stop each one from its row.
- A reload ends both. Some background apps offer to resume afterwards.
