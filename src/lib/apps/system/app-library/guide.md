## Finding an app

- **Discover** shows new and recently updated apps and collections. **All apps** lists everything, with filters, a sort and a grid or list view.
- Search by name, or describe what you need in a sentence: apps that fit show under **Related**. When the browser's built-in AI is available, it adds its picks under **AI suggests**.
- Press Enter in the search box to show the first result's page.

## Installing

- Install keeps an app on this device, in your menus and working offline. Apps you haven't installed still open, but need a connection.
- An app's page also pins it to the desktop or taskbar, adds its widget, marks it a favourite, copies a link that opens it alone in a tab, and uninstalls it.
- **Library** lists what you have, sorted by last use, name or size. **Updates** shows installed apps still downloading after a release, and open apps with a new version ready: **Update** restarts their windows on it.
